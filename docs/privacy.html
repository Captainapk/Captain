Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information our app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

What we collectWhy
Your nameTo personalize your experience and identify you in our rewards program
Your WhatsApp / phone numberTo link your rewards wallet, contact you if needed, and let you reach us directly
Wallet balance and transaction historyTo run our loyalty and rewards program (sign-up bonuses, quiz rewards, redemptions)
Quiz participation and answersTo run quizzes and award correct-answer rewards fairly
Push notification tokenTo send you updates about promotions, quizzes, and announcements
App activity (install date, last active date)To understand engagement and improve the app
Contact button taps (call / WhatsApp)So we know when you've tried to reach us and can follow up

We do not collect your precise location, camera, microphone, or contacts. We do not require a password or traditional account sign-up — only your name and phone number.

How we use your information

  • To operate the app's core features: your rewards wallet, quizzes, promotions, and updates
  • To send you push notifications you can control through your device settings
  • To let our team review and respond to redemption requests and contact attempts
  • To keep the app secure and prevent abuse (for example, blocking accounts that violate fair use)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored using Google Firebase, a cloud platform operated by Google that provides secure data storage and processing. Firebase acts as our data processor and is bound by its own security and privacy standards. You can read Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the rewards program (for example, so your wallet balance and history are preserved even if you reinstall the app). You can request deletion at any time — see "Your choices" below.

Your choices

  • Notifications: you can turn off push notifications at any time in your device's app settings.
  • Delete your data: contact us using the details below and we will delete your name, phone number, and associated activity data, other than records we are required to keep for legitimate business purposes (such as completed transaction records).
  • Questions or corrections: contact us any time using the details below.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to us through the "Get in touch" section in the app, or via the contact details shown on our app's home screen.
